A Universal Shift Register is a versatile sequential circuit capable of performing multiple operations such as shift left, shift right, parallel load, and data storage. It contains flip-flops along with control inputs that determine the mode of operation. Universal shift registers are commonly used in data transfer, data conversion, and digital communication systems.
