An SR Flip-Flop (Set-Reset Flip-Flop) is a basic sequential circuit used to store one bit of information. It has two inputs, S (Set) and R (Reset), which are used to set the output to 1 or reset it to 0. When both inputs are inactive, the flip-flop retains its previous state, while the condition where both inputs are active simultaneously is generally considered invalid.
