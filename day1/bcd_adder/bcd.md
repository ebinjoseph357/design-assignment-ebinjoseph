A BCD (Binary-Coded Decimal) Adder is a digital circuit used to add two BCD digits and produce a valid BCD result. It first performs binary addition and then checks if the sum is greater than 9 or if a carry is generated. If either condition occurs, the circuit adds 0110 (6) to the result to obtain the correct BCD output.
